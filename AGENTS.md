# Model Router operating contract

`auto` is a stable client route that follows complete serving endpoints on its configured hosts. Model names, ports, context limits and concurrency belong to live discovery and serving metadata. Keep deployment identities in configuration.

A temporary catalog failure retains recent evidence until its existing freshness limit. Positive serving evidence reopens a transport circuit. Provider rate limits keep their own cooldown. Recovery keeps one request history entry and preserves the caller connection. Retry only before answer, reasoning or tool output has been delivered. Client disconnects cancel pending work and release admission.

Keep `auto` advertised while a backend restarts. Preserve client permissions, the local-only route boundary, operator controls and unrelated services. Scope automatic full-port discovery to the route's configured hosts.

Expected serving restarts keep connected requests in quiet recovery. An unlimited recovery setting lasts until the caller disconnects. The console shows one route warning after fifteen minutes without serving evidence and clears it when serving returns. Preserve the RSI operator's control of model processes.

Long recovery keeps one request record with a bounded attempt trace and a total attempt count. Catalog health alone must not hide a prolonged failure to generate a response.

Verify backend replacement, metadata changes, temporary failure, cancellation and prevention of output replay. Delivery requires real generation through router host `auto` from the MacBook, plus matching request history. A healthy model catalog alone does not establish delivery.
